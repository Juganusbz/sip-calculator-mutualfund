Why SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to invest a fixed amount regularly (e.g., monthly) into a mutual fund scheme. It's a disciplined approach to investing that helps in wealth creation over the long term through rupee cost averaging and the power of compounding.
